Kiln projects

The projects I have worked on, mostly kilns.

The updraft kiln shed

A small project to house the propane updraft kiln at my studio in Tacoma.

The soda kiln

The project to build and operate a soda kiln at Elinor Maroney's studio in Covington. We have salted the kiln, which means it will forever be a salt/soda kiln. A wide range of results have been delivered into my hands from this project. Expensive failures, dull and bland surfaces, falling flaming doors, and ruined pieces have mostly changed over to exciting textures and dramatic flashes of color. It continues to suffer from being too tall.
